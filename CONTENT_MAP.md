## Content standard
Every commercial article should answer:
1. Who is this for?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What are the important specifications?
4. What are the tradeoffs?
5. What would make us NOT buy it?
6. What alternatives exist?
7. What does ownership cost?
8. Which claims are manufacturer claims?
9. Which observations are first-hand?
10. Where is the affiliate disclosure?
