# Dirt Bike Lab — Build Plan

## Project goal
Build a useful, rider-first affiliate site focused initially on electric dirt bikes, then expand into gear, tools and maintenance.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
1. Choose and register the final domain.
2. Set up managed WordPress or equivalent hosting.
3. Install the site structure.
4. Add About, Privacy, Affiliate Disclosure and Contact pages.
5. Add analytics and Search Console.
6. Apply to Amazon Associates after the site has enough original public content.

Amazon currently says applicants need at least three qualifying sales within the first 180 days for review, and its help page says sites should have robust original content, with at least 10 posts given as a good rule of thumb. See the official Amazon requirements before applying.

## Phase 3 — Monetization
Use multiple revenue sources instead of depending on one retailer:
- Amazon Associates
- Direct manufacturer affiliate programs where available
- Motorcycle/off-road retailers
- Relevant gear brands
- Eventually display advertising after traffic justifies it

## Editorial rule
Do not mass-produce generic AI pages. AI can help research, outline and edit, but the site's differentiator should be first-hand rider knowledge, original photographs, testing notes, measurements and useful comparisons.

## Amazon compliance
Amazon's current Operating Agreement requires the statement:
"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."

Affiliate links also need clear, conspicuous disclosure. Follow the current Amazon agreement and FTC requirements before launch.

## First business metric
Do not optimize for page count. Track:
- Search impressions
- Organic clicks
- Affiliate outbound clicks
- Conversion rate when available
- Revenue per 1,000 visitors
- Revenue per article

## Initial revenue math
We will calculate realistic traffic and commission scenarios after selecting actual products and affiliate programs. Do not use the "$100–$500/day" ad claim as a forecast.
